Cookies Statement

This statement explains how Call the Saul (“we”) uses cookies and similar technologies on callthesaul.com, and the choices you have.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your computer or phone when you visit it. They let the site recognise your device and remember information between pages or visits. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser; persistent cookies stay until they expire or you delete them.

Websites can also keep information in your browser’s own storage (local storage). It works much like a persistent cookie, so this statement covers it too. You can read more about cookies at All About Cookies.

How do we use cookies?

We use only what our website needs to work. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites, to build a profile of you or to show you advertising. The categories are:

  • Necessary. Essential for the website to work, such as remembering your cookie choice. These are always on.
  • Functional. Remember choices you make so the site works the way you set it. We do not use any at present.
  • Performance. Count visits and show how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not use any at present.
  • Targeting. Used to show advertising that matches your interests. We do not use any.
  • Social media. Set by social networks to let you share content. We do not use any.

If we ever want to add performance or functional cookies, we will update this statement first and ask for your choice again. We will not rely on a choice you made before.

What are your options if you do not want cookies on your device?

When you first visit our website, we ask for your choice before using any cookies that are not strictly necessary. You can change your choice at any time with the ‘Cookie settings’ link in the footer of our website, or with the button below.

You can also set your browser to block cookies and site data, or to alert you before they are stored, and you can delete what is already stored. If you block necessary storage, parts of our website may not remember your choices. Your browser’s help pages explain how:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
